English frequency dictionary (en_82765.txt.gz)
================================================

Source: the SymSpell project by Wolf Garbe
  https://github.com/wolfgarbe/SymSpell
  (SymSpell/frequency_dictionary_en_82_765.txt, repository licensed MIT)

The dictionary was created by intersecting two data sources, and their
terms apply to the word/frequency data itself:

  - Google Books Ngram data — word frequencies.
    Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0):
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
    Attribution: Google Books Ngram Viewer / Google Inc.

  - SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) by Kevin Atkinson —
    genuine-vocabulary filter. Permissive license:
    http://wordlist.aspell.net/scowl-readme/

Modifications made here: removed the UTF-8 byte-order mark and
gzip-compressed the file. The word/frequency content is unchanged.

The SymSpellKt library (com.darkrockstudios:symspellkt) that consumes
this dictionary is licensed MIT:
